    Models, receiver bindings, calibration, flight counts and per-model preferences are preserved. Nothing is sent to GitHub.

    Recovery & reconnect

    If the browser closes or a drive disconnects, reconnect the same radio, load the release, select both drives and your existing backup folder. Before firmware copying, use Restore SD files to recover the previous program files. After a firmware attempt, confirm the reconnect and run the final check; the installer will not automatically retry the flash.

    SD-only restore is blocked after firmware copying. If you need to roll back, use your original backup and the package instructions to restore firmware and SD files together.
